Company Expense Reimbursement Policy

Travel Expenses:
Employees can claim reimbursement for travel expenses up to 5000 INR per trip.
Eligible travel categories include flights, train tickets, taxis, and hotel stays.

Meal Expenses:
Meal expenses are reimbursable up to 2000 INR per day.
Alcohol expenses are not reimbursable.

Office Supplies:
Office supply expenses are reimbursable up to 3000 INR per purchase.

Approval Rules:
All reimbursements above 3000 INR require manager approval.

Required Documents:
Receipt or invoice must be submitted for reimbursement processing.

Processing Timeline:
Approved reimbursements will be processed within 7 business days.
